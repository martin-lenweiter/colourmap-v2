Visual universe goal
--------------------

The aim is not to create isolated illustrations. The aim is to build a coherent
Colourmap education visual universe: warm, hopeful, quiet, human, symbolic, and
recognisable across every comic program.

For Room to Breathe, panels should feel like one continuous inner landscape:

- same main character silhouette or emotional point of view
- same warm dark palette with ochre, cream, muted blue, and soft green accents
- same hand-made comic texture, graphite/ink line weight, paper grain, and cinematic lighting
- no baked-in text, letters, labels, signs, or UI words
- simple compositions with space for app-rendered text
- one clear emotional idea per panel
- hope and spaciousness as the final emotional direction

Future education comics should share this visual DNA, while changing symbols and
settings per topic.

Canonical style:

Sober hopeful European BD on aged beige paper, visible paper grain, hand-drawn graphite/ink lines,
subtle crosshatching, muted watercolor/gouache washes, ochre light, dusty blue shadows, sage/teal
accents, calm human figures, and poetic symbolic environments. Uplifting but not cute.

Warm/cold language:

Warm tones are preferred as the emotional home base. Cold tones can appear when the mind feels
distant, abstract, crowded, or uncertain, but the journey should move toward warmth, groundedness,
and breathable space.

Emotional direction:

Images should lift the viewer. They can show difficulty, but should not immerse the user in
melancholy or sadness. Prefer hope, space, dignity, tenderness, agency, and quiet courage.

Old paper / future signal:

Keep the aged handmade paper feeling, but allow subtle futuristic elements when useful: luminous
dots, soft emotional maps, abstract constellations, or quiet data-like fields. The future mood
should sit inside the handmade drawing, not replace it.
