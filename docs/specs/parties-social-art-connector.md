# Parties — Art as the Social Connector

**Status:** Idea-stage, logged 2026-04-24. Big vision, not scheduled.

## The idea

Colourmap hosts / powers **parties** where the app becomes the social fabric.

At an event (a private party, a Colourmap-organized gathering, a festival corner):

- **Guests download the app** to enter the experience
- **The music is collaborative** — guests can influence what's playing by mixing stems, voting, adding pieces (ties into ColourStudios, Vol. II)
- **Visuals react to their phones** — the projected art responds to the collective rhythm, like a shared pulse
- **Contacts exchange through art** — you discover someone at the party because their "colour" matches yours, not because you swiped
- **Art projections** light the space — custom visuals tied to the music and the crowd's state

The app becomes a *social membrane* at a physical event. Art is the connector.

## Why this is different

- Not another Shazam / Spotify "now playing" at parties
- Not a dating app
- Not a ticketing app
- It's a **shared aesthetic space** — people meet because they contribute to the same thing

## Physical dimensions

- Projection screens / mapping in venue
- Bluetooth low-energy beacon for presence detection (who's in the room)
- Audio played through venue sound system, driven by the app's mix
- Phones optional as personal instruments — wave them, they visualize

## Business angle

- Event hosts pay to license the tool for their party
- Colourmap-branded parties in key cities become marketing + community + revenue
- Art partners earn (visual artists contributing projections)
- Scales into festival residencies / corporate event space

## Minimum viable party

1. Small gathering (20 people) with one projector
2. Background music via Relax Sounds, mood pulled from group check-in
3. Big "add your stem" moment where 3 volunteers contribute
4. Simple "who's here tonight" screen showing initials + colours

## Open questions

- How do you prevent bad actors from DDOS'ing the mix?
- Voting / curator model to keep music coherent?
- Venue tech requirements — do we ship a hardware kit?
- Privacy model for "find contacts" — opt-in only
