# Three-App Architecture

**Status:** Vision / Strategic Spec  
**Date:** 2026-04-28

---

## Overview

Colourmap is not one product — it is three distinct, deeply connected apps that share a unified identity and a common thread: *the felt sense of being alive, present, and connected to others.*

Each app serves a different mode of human experience. They feel different, look different, and attract different moments in a person's day. But they all share the same underlying language: colour, presence, and resonance.

---

## The Three Apps

### 1. Daily — *"Who am I today?"*

The core app. FDS (Feeling · Doing · Sharing) + teamwork layer.

**What it is:**  
A daily rhythm engine. Check in with your mood. See your objectives. Track what you're doing. Notice how you're relating to others. Over time, patterns emerge — weeks of feeling, doing, sharing that paint a picture of a life.

**Core surfaces:**
- Feeling tab — mood register, binaural tuner, inner weather
- Doing tab — objectives, quick tasks, agenda, daily inbox
- Sharing tab — Lonely → Connected axis, circle health
- Road / Overview — life categories, progress, vision anchor, mastery
- Teamwork layer (later) — shared missions with circles, collaborative objectives

**Who uses it:**  
Everyone. The base layer. Morning, evening. Quiet check-ins or deep dives. The person who wants to live intentionally.

**Vibe:** Warm, serif, organic. Like a journal that knows you.

---

### 2. Sparks & Chat — *"Who's around me?"*

The social presence layer. Real-time vibes, short messages, emotional weather between people.

**What it is:**  
A low-pressure social feed where people share sparks — short emotional transmissions (mood, a thought, a sound, a colour). Plus a direct chat layer for circles and individuals. Not a social media feed. Not a messenger. Something in between: presence without performance.

**Core surfaces:**
- Sparks feed — what's alive in your circles right now
- DM / Circle chat — direct, intimate, warm
- Emitter dot — broadcast your vibe to people who are receptive
- Connection pulse — who resonates with you this week?

**Who uses it:**  
The relational user. Someone who wants to feel their people without the noise of social media. Friend groups, couples, small crews.

**Vibe:** Lighter, more ephemeral. Fast, fluid. Like passing notes in a class you both love.

---

### 3. Creative Lab — *"What can we make?"*

The sound and creation space. Groove Machine, Binaural Tuner, collective music experiences, visual experiments.

**What it is:**  
A co-creation layer where people build soundscapes, grooves, and experiences together. The Groove Machine and Binaural Tuner live here in their full depth. Collective sessions (later) let groups shape a shared sonic environment. The lab is where individual tools become collective instruments.

**Core surfaces:**
- Groove Machine — individual and collective beat creation
- Binaural Tuner — personal sound environments
- Sound Studio — sample packs, layered composition (later)
- Collective Sessions — shared groove, live vibe sync (later)
- Visual Lab — atom visualizers, wave shapes, live visuals (later)

**Who uses it:**  
The maker. The person who wants to zone into music. The circle who wants to vibe together. The brand or event using colourmap as an experience layer.

**Vibe:** Darker, more immersive. Studio energy. Sacred geometry. The focused mind.

---

## Cross-App Connections

These three apps are not siloed — they bleed into each other by design:

| Signal | From | To |
|--------|------|----|
| Feeling check-in → Groove Machine suggests a preset | Daily | Creative Lab |
| Sparks feed picks up collective groove state | Creative Lab | Sparks |
| Circle health score influences sharing check-in prompt | Sparks | Daily |
| Mastery goals show up in teamwork missions | Daily | Daily (Road ↔ Doing) |
| A shared groove session creates a spark in the feed | Creative Lab | Sparks |

---

## Unified Identity

All three apps share:
- The same serif + warm palette design language
- The same FDS axis as the emotional backbone (Feeling · Doing · Sharing)
- The same circle/people graph (who you're connected to)
- The same account (one login, one person, three windows into their world)

The apps are not separate downloads. They are three tabs, three modes, three doors into the same lived experience.

---

## Phasing

**Phase 1 (now):** Daily is the primary app. Sparks and Chat exist as early features. Creative Lab tools exist (Groove Machine, Binaural Tuner) but are not yet a full separate surface.

**Phase 2:** Sparks becomes a real feed with presence indicators. Creative Lab gets a dedicated entry point. Collective sessions (shared grooves) ship.

**Phase 3:** The three surfaces converge — a unified experience where your daily mood, your social presence, and your creative output are all visible in one flow.
