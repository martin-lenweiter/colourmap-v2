# Billy Pineapple First 100 Pages

## Goal

Define the first 100 pages of the Billy Pineapple infinite comic so image generation can scale
without losing the story spine.

V1 principle:

```text
Funny journey first.
Deep symbolic structure underneath.
Images are reusable.
Text adapts later.
```

## Story Arc

The first 100 pages are one complete first journey:

```text
Home
-> too many directions
-> first crossing
-> suspicious guides
-> Groovy Jungle
-> Ancestral Pyramid
-> Crocodile Jack's influence
-> Ocean of Sadness
-> Desert of Loneliness
-> Babylon glimpse
-> Observatory return
```

The question underneath:

```text
Is The Juice a thing Billy finds, or a way Billy learns to move through worlds?
```

## Page Outline

### 1-10: Leaving Pineapple Planet

1. Billy at the pineapple home doorway with backpack and empty cup.
2. Wooden arrow crossroads with symbolic icons and too many directions.
3. Billy crosses the first river on an unreliable bridge.
4. Hooded stranger with cup lantern at the split path.
5. Galaxy Gummies glowing in the jungle path.
6. Crocodile Jack's first friendly juice stand.
7. Billy backs away and hears drums from Groovy Jungle.
8. Funky monkeys lead Billy toward the Ancestral Pyramid.
9. Billy meets triangle pineapple prophets at the pyramid entrance.
10. Billy looks back and sees home tiny in the distance.

### 11-20: Ancestral Pyramid

11. Stone door opens into a golden rhythm chamber.
12. Triangle pineapple prophets arranged in a circle of drums.
13. Billy's empty cup placed in the center of the chamber.
14. Funky monkeys interrupt a sacred ceremony with snacks.
15. A wall mural shows pineapples searching for Juice across ages.
16. Billy sees a shadow pineapple version of himself.
17. Crocodile Jack appears in a carved wall as if he has always been there.
18. A prophet points to three doorways: sun, river, and city.
19. Billy chooses the river doorway.
20. The pyramid floor becomes a flowing map.

### 21-30: River Of Regret

21. Billy floats in a tiny cup-shaped boat.
22. Old choices appear as doors on the riverbanks.
23. Billy sees a version of himself who never left the sofa.
24. Billy sees a version who became too optimized.
25. Funny poll scene: is Square Billy obscure?
26. A bridge forms from unfinished planks.
27. Billy drops one Galaxy Gummy into the water.
28. The water shows Crocodile Jack selling fake maps.
29. Billy reaches a dock with blues music in the distance.
30. The river opens into the Ocean of Sadness.

### 31-40: Ocean Of Sadness

31. Billy stands on Blue Lantern Dock.
32. Old musicians play blues on floating platforms.
33. A whale passes under the dock, carrying reflected stars.
34. Billy sits without trying to solve anything.
35. Rain falls, but rays of light break through.
36. Funny scene: Billy tries to interview a very serious pelican.
37. Crocodile Jack offers bottled sadness as premium depth.
38. Billy refuses and listens to the music instead.
39. A lantern boat appears for the Desert of Loneliness.
40. Billy follows one ray of light toward shore.

### 41-50: Desert Of Loneliness

41. Billy enters warm empty dunes with a small cup shadow.
42. Phone signals appear as mirages.
43. A long table with one chair sits in the sand.
44. Billy tries to talk to an echo that gives bad advice.
45. The hooded stranger reappears as a distant campfire figure.
46. Billy lights a tiny campfire with one honest signal.
47. Other small lights appear far away.
48. Funny poll: would you trust the Friendly Tomato?
49. Billy meets Friendly Tomato and cannot read the vibe.
50. Footprints become a road toward Babylon.

### 51-60: First Babylon Glimpse

51. Babylon appears as a beautiful solarpunk city.
52. Billy rides a silent train through gardens and towers.
53. Colourmap phone readout shows "searching" mood weather.
54. Billy receives a tiny office badge from an unknown department.
55. Rachel requests a report; no one knows Rachel.
56. Crocodile Jack appears as a consultant with perfect teeth.
57. The city shows real gifts: clean water, care systems, libraries.
58. The city also shows quiet citizens staring at the horizon.
59. Billy sees a hidden door marked only by a pineapple icon.
60. The door leads to the Forbidden Basement Disco.

### 61-70: Basement Disco And System Trap

61. Billy enters a warm underground disco under Babylon.
62. Citizens dance like they have rediscovered their bodies.
63. Crocodile Jack tries to sponsor the dance floor.
64. Funny poll: is this party liberation or avoidance?
65. Billy dances badly but sincerely.
66. The floor becomes a map of systems and desires.
67. Jack turns the music into a productivity jingle.
68. The dancers freeze into efficient choreography.
69. Billy spills his empty cup, breaking the rhythm spell.
70. A tunnel opens back toward Groovy Jungle.

### 71-80: Return To Groovy Jungle

71. Billy returns carrying Babylon dust on his shoes.
72. The monkeys inspect the dust like scholars.
73. Triangle pineapple prophets ask no questions and somehow judge him.
74. Billy sees that creativity can become consumption too.
75. Galaxy Gummies grow on vines and sing quietly.
76. Crocodile Jack tries to bottle the jungle rhythm.
77. Billy and monkeys hide the rhythm inside a drum circle.
78. A bridge of sound appears above the jungle canopy.
79. Billy climbs toward a mountain of unknown direction.
80. The mountain path becomes steep and symbolic.

### 81-90: Mountain Of The Unknown

81. Billy climbs with the empty cup tied to his backpack.
82. The hooded stranger walks ahead, then becomes Billy's silhouette.
83. Billy meets Square Billy on a ledge.
84. Funny poll: how obscure is Square Billy?
85. Square Billy offers a shortcut made of perfect angles.
86. Billy tries it and gets stuck in a corner.
87. A ray from Ocean of Sadness and a drumbeat from Groovy Jungle free him.
88. Billy reaches a bridge between mountain peaks.
89. Crocodile Jack waits on the bridge with a golden contract.
90. Billy crosses without signing.

### 91-100: The Observatory

91. Billy arrives at a quiet Observatory above all regions.
92. The map below shows Pineapple Planet, Babylon, Groovy Jungle, Ocean, Desert.
93. Billy sees every region has gift and risk.
94. The empty cup glows but does not fill.
95. Billy laughs because this feels both profound and annoying.
96. Colourmap shows not an answer, but a wider map.
97. Friends' paths appear as tiny lights in different regions.
98. Billy earns the first Journey Medal.
99. The home sofa appears in the Observatory as a doorway.
100. Billy returns home wider, and a new leaf opens on Pineapple Planet.

## First Image Batch Rule

Generate in batches of 8-12 panels. After every batch:

- inspect for baked text
- check phone readability
- check lower overlay area
- check Billy consistency
- check Crocodile Jack consistency
- decide which images can become reusable loops
