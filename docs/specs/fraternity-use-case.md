# Fraternity Use Case

**Status:** Concept / Use Case Spec  
**Date:** 2026-04-28

---

## What It Is

A fraternity — or any tight-knit crew, sports team, friend group, or house — is one of the most intense forms of small-group social life that exists. These are people who eat together, train together, compete together, sometimes live together. Their collective emotional weather matters. Their bonds are the thing.

Colourmap fits this context with surprising precision.

---

## The Problem This Solves

Right now, fraternities and similar crews communicate through group chats (WhatsApp, iMessage, Discord). These tools are great for logistics but terrible for:

- **Sensing each other's mood** — is the vibe off tonight or is it just me?
- **Tracking collective energy across a semester** — are we burning out? Are we riding high?
- **Coordinating shared intentions** — who's working toward what? Who needs support?
- **Marking moments** — game wins, parties, hard weeks, celebration peaks

Colourmap becomes the *emotional and social layer* beneath the logistics chat.

---

## How They'd Use It

### Daily Presence
Each brother checks in — a quick FDS scan. Feeling: where am I emotionally. Doing: what's my priority today. Sharing: how connected do I feel to the group right now.

This takes 30 seconds. But the aggregate across a house of 20 shows the collective field: "9 people are feeling scattered today," "The house is at its highest sharing score in 3 weeks."

### Sparks Feed
Short vibes, quick transmissions. "Energy on the court today was different." "Tough exam done." "Who's around tonight?" Not posts — sparks. They fade. They're real-time presence, not performance.

### Circle Health
The group is a Circle. A shared score tracks the health of the collective relationship over time. Not gamified — felt. When the score dips, a soft prompt surfaces: "It's been 12 days since the group had a high sharing score. What's going on?"

### Collective Events
Before a big game, before a party, before an initiation — open a shared Groove Machine session. Everyone's phone feeds into a shared beat. The energy of the room shapes the groove. This is the creative lab meeting the social layer.

After the event, log it. A "Slow Win" — a note, a spark, a mood snapshot. The house's emotional history accumulates.

### Teamwork Layer (Future)
Shared missions: "House GPA above 3.2 this semester." "Every brother does one social event per week." Individual objectives roll up to the group's collective score. Not competitive — collaborative.

---

## Why It Works

Fraternities are built on:
1. **Shared identity** — colourmap reflects this through the visual language of circles, dots, colour
2. **Emotional bonds** — FDS checks in with the felt sense that makes brotherhood real
3. **Collective rituals** — check-ins become a ritual, the collective groove becomes ceremony
4. **Discretion** — not a public social media feed; a private, intimate layer

Colourmap doesn't replace the brotherhood. It makes it visible to itself.

## Positioning

This is not a "productivity tool for fraternities." That misses the point.

This is: **a felt-sense layer for your most important relationships.** The people who matter most get a window into your inner weather. You get a window into theirs.

Colourmap for fraternities is Colourmap for any tight crew. The use case scales to:
- Sports teams (pre/post game emotional tracking)
- Music bands and collectives
- Startup founding teams
- Intentional living communities
- Close friend groups navigating life transitions together
