# Billy Pineapple: New Babylon 150-Page Arc

## Aim

New Babylon is the first major Billy Pineapple city arc. It should feel like a funny cartoon about a
pineapple getting a job, then slowly reveal a deeper question about AI, progress, work, desire, and
The Juice.

The reader should relax first. Then, suddenly, a page should make them think:

```text
What if the future gives us paradise, but we are still too busy chasing Juice to live in it?
```

The arc is about AI hope and AI shadow at the same time:

- **AI Hope**: better tools, creative freedom, medical care, education, less boring work, beautiful
  cities, abundance, time returned to humans.
- **AI Shadow**: productivity slavery, job anxiety, hollow optimization, fake wellness, algorithmic
  desire, artificial Juice, and corporations turning meaning into a subscription.
- **Fine Balance**: AI is not the villain. The question is who owns the machine, what humans do with
  the freedom, and whether paradise becomes another dashboard.
- **Progress Truth**: the arc should keep asking what real progress is. The answer cannot be only
  speed, profit, automation, or comfort. Real progress must keep justice, art, love, shared causes,
  dignity, and time to actually live. The robots, dashboards, and Juice systems should show both
  sides: they can free people, and they can also trap people when Crocodile Corp owns the meaning.
- Fork cadence should be quick: major questions or branch gates every 3-6 pages max. The comic can
  later add alternate pages behind those gates.

## Core Question

The deepest question of the arc:

```text
What is The Juice?
```

New Babylon's version:

```text
If AI, automation, apps, luxury, productivity, and perfect systems produce endless Juice,
why does nobody feel like there is enough?
```

## Tone

- funny first
- wise underneath
- playful satire, never boring lesson content
- symbolic darkness allowed, but not gore or misery porn
- zombies can appear as funny productivity zombies, not horror monsters
- Crocodile Jack remains mysterious and stable, not overused
- Crocodile Corp is funny, charming, dangerous, and spiritually empty

## Visual Rules

- portrait phone pages
- no speech bubbles or captions baked into images unless explicitly listed as signage
- no borders or white frames
- leave calm lower space when possible for app text overlay
- normal pages are more cartoony and readable
- intersection pages can become more watercolor/concept-art with reflections, fountains, bridges,
  giant machines, city views, or symbolic doors
- use a unified cartoon-watercolor style: simple readable characters, soft paper texture, hopeful
  light, and occasional cinematic concept-art depth at major gates
- some pages may hide funny visual details in the lower overlay area so the user discovers them
  after tapping through the text layer
- use written signage sparingly only when it is world identity:
  - `NEW BABYLON`
  - `CROCODILE CORP`
  - `SOLVING THE WORLD ONE BITE AT A TIME`

## Costume Continuity

Billy changes clothes by timeline/role so the reader can recognize branches.

- **Default Billy**: simple pineapple, sneakers, expressive eyes, maybe backpack.
- **Groovy Billy**: flower shirt, relaxed posture, sometimes sunglasses, jungle/music scenes.
- **Worker Billy**: tiny office badge, shirt/tie or serious worker clothes, tired eyes, no backpack.
- **Luxury Billy**: vacation shirt, tiny drink umbrella, overwhelmed by paradise options.
- **Crime Path Billy**: darker jacket or suspicious little tie, still funny and not too cool.
- **Fork Rule**: when a timeline forks, Billy keeps the outfit for that branch for several pages.
  When the story returns to a hub, he can return to default Billy.

## New Babylon

New Babylon is not a grey dystopia. It is beautiful:

- solarpunk towers
- gardens
- palm trees
- AI trains
- public fountains
- rivers of Juice
- rooftop parties
- libraries
- wellness spas
- music plazas
- luxury jacuzzis
- open sky
- pineapple robots
- funny recurring fruit/vegetable citizens

The joke and tragedy:

```text
Paradise is everywhere, but everybody is too busy optimizing paradise to enjoy it.
```

At the center is the **Fantastic Juice Machine**:

- giant public machine/fountain
- rivers and fountains of Juice flow from it
- people worship, analyze, monetize, and queue around it
- Crocodile Corp claims it can improve the world by controlling the Juice supply
- Crocodile Corp eventually creates **Artificial Juice**: smoother, cheaper, more addictive, and
  never quite enough

## Crocodile Corp

Company identity:

```text
CROCODILE CORP
Solving the world one bite at a time.
```

Crocodile Corp is not just evil. It is seductive because it works:

- it makes useful apps
- it builds beautiful offices
- it pays Billy in Juice coupons
- it speaks the language of purpose
- it promises to solve hunger, loneliness, productivity, love, and sadness
- it turns every problem into a dashboard

But it always wants more:

- more Juice
- more users
- more efficiency
- more attention
- more emotional data
- more unpaid interns shaped like fruit

## Parody Characters

Use fictional parody, not documentary likeness.

- **Jeff Bananos**: a space/shop billionaire archetype. Smiling, bald-ish, delivery drones,
  rocket warehouses, jungle supply chains. Do not make him literally Jeff Bezos.
- **Elon Dusk**: rocket/electric/AI tycoon parody. Dramatic cape or engineering jacket, obsessed
  with tunnels, robots, Mars-flavored Juice, and posting through crises.
- **President Donald Grump**: orange cartoon president of New Babylon. Loud, ridiculous, corrupt in
  the fictional world, doing business with Crocodile Corp. Political satire, not a factual claim
  about a real person.

Use Donald Grump and Elon Dusk sparingly. They are short satirical flashes, not main cast.

## Recurring Comic Cast

- **Tomato Guy**: Billy's friend. Warm, slightly suspicious-looking by accident, but genuinely good.
- **Banana Guy**: very happy good guy with sunglasses. He appears in crowds, parties, and hopeful
  scenes, often smiling too much.
- **Potato People**: round, grounded, practical citizens. Funny background workers, neighbors, and
  commuters.
- **Pineapple Robots**: New Babylon helper robots. Some are sweet, some are too efficient, and some
  are quietly confused by human desire.
- Background cameos matter: characters can reappear later as clues, jokes, or emotional anchors.

## Branch Structure

New Babylon should not be one straight path. It should contain multiple roads that later reveal
funny links if the reader plays both timelines.

Main branches:

1. **Good Time Road**
   - Billy enjoys parties, fountains, jacuzzis, pineapple girls, rooftop music, AI chefs, and city
     wonders.
   - He thinks he has found The Juice.
   - The hidden problem: pleasure without presence becomes another job.

2. **Worker Road**
   - Billy joins Crocodile Corp.
   - He learns vibe coding, productivity apps, AI dashboards, wellness OKRs, and infinite meetings.
   - The hidden problem: he works to create paradise, but misses paradise outside the window.

3. **Crime Road**
   - Crocodile Corp controls the Fantastic Juice Machine.
   - Some characters become Juice dealers, selling premium access to meaning.
   - Billy is tempted to join because it finally feels like power.
   - The hidden problem: controlling The Juice destroys the possibility of receiving it.

4. **Observer Road**
   - Billy notices parallel versions of himself across the city.
   - One Billy is partying, one is working, one is selling Juice, one is staring at the sunset.
   - The hidden problem: every path has a gift and a cost.

Funny cross-links:

- Party Billy sees Worker Billy through an office window and thinks, "that fruit needs a vacation."
- Worker Billy sees Party Billy from the office and thinks, "that fruit is wasting potential."
- Crime Billy sells bottled Juice to both.
- Observer Billy realizes all three are thirsty.

### Crocodile Jack Side Timeline

New Babylon should eventually include a separate Crocodile Jack timeline: a stylish, cinematic
"day in the life" and later a flashback rise-to-power arc. This should feel like a Guy Ritchie crime
film translated into Pineapple Planet language: cool, funny, dangerous, visually rich, but still
symbolic rather than generic gangster cosplay.

This is not the main Billy path at first. It is a contrast layer the user can later unlock, then
recognize from Billy's perspective in another timeline.

Core idea:

```text
Crocodile Jack did not simply appear at the top of New Babylon.
He learned how desire moves, then built roads around it.
```

Visual ingredients:

- golden crocodile-style limousine
- crocodile-shaped motorbike and shady motorbike gangs
- leather boots, rings, dark floral shirts, heavy coats, stylish sunglasses
- piranha aquariums in deep executive offices
- secret lair under Crocodile Corp with Juice valves, money stashes, old contracts, and wanted
  posters
- rusty bars, back rooms, neon alleys, cyberpunk markets, hidden fighting rooms
- shady fruit characters with strong silhouettes: piranha dealers, mango bookies, lemon drivers,
  pineapple bouncers, potato fixers, tomato informants, crocodile lieutenants
- barehanded underground fights where Crocodile Jack watches Billy struggle and later become a
  champion in a different branch

Tone rules:

- cool and badass, but not empty violence
- stylish crime energy, but still phone-friendly and comic-symbolic
- no gore
- fights are about pressure, pride, fear, discipline, and survival, not cruelty
- women should be present as powerful operators, fighters, drivers, strategists, journalists, and
  rivals, not decoration
- Crocodile Jack stays charming, intelligent, and dangerous; his evil is extraction through style,
  not random brutality

Future branch structure:

1. **Day In The Life Of Jack**
   - Jack wakes in luxury above New Babylon.
   - Golden limousine through palm-neon streets.
   - Piranha aquarium office.
   - Motorbike ride through the shady Juice district.
   - Rusty bar meeting with strange fruit operators.
   - Underground fight night where Billy appears from another timeline.
   - Secret lair below Crocodile Corp.
   - Jack studies the Fantastic Juice Machine like a priest studying a false god.

2. **Young Jack Flashbacks**
   - young Crocodile Jack as a street hustler in early New Babylon
   - first discovery that desire can be packaged
   - first motorbike gang
   - first betrayal
   - first Artificial Juice experiment
   - first time he realizes friendly power works better than obvious violence
   - rise from back rooms to Crocodile Corp

3. **Billy Contrast Timeline**
   - Billy enters the fighting world from his own angle.
   - Jack watches him struggle before understanding him as useful or dangerous.
   - The same scenes from Jack's timeline later reappear with different meaning in Billy's path.
   - The reader realizes New Babylon is not one story, but overlapping roads.

The deeper question of this branch:

```text
When does charisma become capture?
When does survival become extraction?
When does style hide hunger?
```

## Image Generation Batch Rule

Generate in batches of 10-15. After each batch:

- inspect for baked text mistakes
- reject accidental real-person likeness if too direct
- check costume continuity
- check lower overlay calm area
- reject panels that feel overloaded: one clear action, relaxed visual density, and only a few
  background clues should be the default
- alternate density across the batch: if one scene is visually loaded, the next should breathe
- pineapple girls should read as pineapple beings first, not humans with pineapple hair
- compress to WebP for phone loading
- update `lib/billy-comic.ts`
