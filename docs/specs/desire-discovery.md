# Desires — Shared Intentions Inside Your Circles

> "I want to go to the lake this weekend."
> A friend in your Circle says "I'm in."
> A plan forms.

**Status:** Spec 2026-04-27. Not yet built.

---

## The Idea

You already have Circles — small groups of people you trust.
What's missing is a way to say **what you want to do** and find
out who in your network wants the same thing.

Not a chat. Not a task. A **desire** — an intention you put into
the air, available for anyone in your Circles to resonate with.

Works for:
- **Fun** — lake trip, ping pong, slow Sunday breakfast somewhere new
- **Creative** — "want to collab on a track", "looking for someone to
  paint with", "building a short film, need a DP"
- **Professional** — "looking for a co-founder", "want to find a
  client together", "building something in public, who's in"
- **Personal growth** — "starting a morning run habit, anyone?",
  "want to read this book with someone"

---

## How It Works

### 1. Post a desire
Inside any Circle (or as a cross-Circle broadcast to all your Circles),
you write a short intention:

```
"go to the lake this weekend"
"collab on a track — something slow and textured"
"looking for a co-founder, building in fintech"
"morning runs, 7am, anyone?"
```

Optional: time window (this week / this month / no rush), category
(fun / creative / professional / growth).

### 2. Friends respond
Members of your Circles see the desire. They can:
- **Resonate** ("I'm in" / "interested") — shows up as a soft signal,
  not a full commitment yet
- **Comment** — one line, keep it light: "Saturday works, which lake?"
- **Ignore** — no pressure, no visible rejection

### 3. A plan or sub-circle forms
Once 2+ people resonate:
- The desire becomes a lightweight plan thread
- You can set a date, share a location, loop in more people
- If it's recurring or ongoing (co-founder search, creative collab),
  it can graduate into a full Circle of its own

---

## Where Desires Live

Desires sit between missions and the social log.

- **Missions** = things the group has committed to doing together
- **Desires** = things one person wants that they're opening up to others
- **Log** = what happened

A desire becomes a mission once people commit. Until then it stays
soft — an invitation, not an obligation.

---

## Cross-Circle Visibility

You can scope a desire:
- **One Circle** — only the people in that group see it
- **All my Circles** — visible to everyone across your Circles
- (No public feed — desires stay within your trusted network)

This is the key mechanic: your desires travel across your whole
social graph without leaving it. You might post "collab on a track"
to all Circles and find out that someone in your work Circle is
also a musician.

---

## Emitters and Receivers

Some people post desires constantly — they're the initiators, the
ones who pull others into motion. Others rarely post but always say
yes. Both are essential. The mechanic works because of both.

No labels. No scores. Just the natural rhythm of who initiates
and who responds.

---

## Connection to Existing Features

- **Circles** — desires live inside them; the trust is already there
- **Missions** — a desire that gets commitment becomes a mission
- **Agenda** — accepted plans land on the shared 14-day strip
- **Compass / feelings** — what you desire is connected to your inner
  state; someone in Courage posts different desires than someone in
  Peace

## Layer 2 — The Activity Map (Open to Outsiders)

Some desires are open. The person posting says:
> "I'm going to the lake Saturday — open to others joining."

When a desire is marked **open**, it appears on a map.

Anyone in the city can see it. They see:
- The activity ("lake trip")
- The zone / neighbourhood (not exact address until accepted)
- The time window
- How many people are already in
- The compass colour of the person who posted it (their vibe, if shared)

They do NOT see: full name, face, links, history.

### Joining an Open Activity

- Tap the pin → read the desire → tap "I want to join"
- The poster gets a soft notification: "someone nearby wants to join"
- The poster can accept or ignore (no visible rejection to the asker)
- If accepted → a lightweight thread opens between them → they can
  coordinate, and optionally form a Circle afterward

### Zone Mapping (Not Exact Location)

Desires appear on the map as **zones**, not precise pins:
- "Near Canal Saint-Martin" not "47 Quai de Valmy"
- Exact location shared only after mutual acceptance
- This keeps it safe and prevents the map from feeling like surveillance

### What the Map Shows

Each open activity is a soft dot on the map:
- Colour = category (fun / creative / professional / growth)
- Size = how many people are already in
- Pulse = recent activity (someone just joined, or desire was just posted)

No feed. No algorithm. Just a living map of what people near you
want to do and are open to doing together.

### Who Can Post to the Map

Not everyone. To keep quality high:
- You need at least one active Circle (you're already part of a trusted
  network — the map is an extension of that, not a replacement)
- Desires are posted from inside a Circle first, then optionally
  opened to the map
- This means every open activity has at least one real social anchor —
  it's not coming from nowhere

---

## The Full Picture

```
Your Circles (private)
    ↓ post a desire
Visible to people you know
    ↓ optionally mark "open"
Appears on the city map
    ↓ someone nearby sees it
They request to join
    ↓ you accept
Lightweight thread → coordinate
    ↓ optionally
New Circle forms — strangers become trusted
```

The map is not a social network. It's a **surface for shared action**.
You meet through doing, not through browsing profiles.
