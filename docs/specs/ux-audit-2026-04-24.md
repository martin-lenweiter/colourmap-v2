# UX / UI audit — 2026-04-24

Audit of the app from a "lay person" perspective: someone who isn't a dev,
doesn't know Colourmap's vocabulary, and lands on the site for the first
time — or opens it on a phone after a long day.

---

## Phone view (primary surface)

### What works now
- **Check-in tab** is the right home screen — one clear action ("how are you?")
  with three axes (body / focus / clarity) as big visible bars.
- **Calming Sounds** under the Sounds tab is instantly engaging — a big
  wave, play button, genre pills, layered real recordings. Even without
  reading anything, people tap pills and hear results.
- **Keyboard-aware wrapper** now applied to CheckInForm + CircleBoard.
- **`min-h-svh`** on containers and body prevents bottom-chrome jitter.

### What hurts on phone
1. **Sounds tab still feels dense on first load.** The wave + picker + genre
   pills + volume slider + layer cards stack vertically and fill >1.5
   screens. First-time user scrolls past the wave before understanding
   what "beat frequency" means.
   **Fix:** collapse *everything below the wave* into an "expand" drawer by
   default. Only show wave + play + one genre row on the first screen.
2. **Feeling card vertical bars** (body / focus / clarity) are ~18px tall
   each, plus the losange on the side. On a small phone (iPhone SE) the
   bars are hard to hit precisely — index lands on wrong cell.
   **Fix:** bump to 24px min height on mobile, and widen the tap surface by
   letting the whole row listen, not just the bar.
3. **Circles first-time UX** is opaque — empty state shows "How Circles
   works" below the fold. Users land, see nothing, tap back.
   **Fix:** move the "How Circles works" explainer to be the *empty state
   card* itself, with a big "create your first circle" CTA under it.
4. **Tab bar letters** (Day / Circles / Notebook) have no icons. On a phone
   people scan icons first.
   **Fix:** add minimalist glyphs — a half-moon for Day, three dots for
   Circles, an underlined line for Notebook. Keep the text labels, small.
5. **Calming Sounds "open studio" pill** is the right gesture but it moves
   a lot of content in/out at once. Disorienting.
   **Fix:** when transitioning to studio, slide (not swap) the extra panels
   in with a 250ms stagger. Keeps the wave + play where the eye expects.
6. **No haptic feedback** on any primary control. On iOS especially, a
   tiny `navigator.vibrate(10)` on genre pills + play button would make it
   feel responsive. (Wrapper deferred from earlier work.)

### What's missing on phone
- **Quick-start flow for new users.** First-ever load shows a three-screen
  intro: "you're doing a self check-in → sounds support your state → over
  time this becomes your map." Dismissable, stored in localStorage.
- **Persistent "are you feeling it" ping.** After 2 days without a
  check-in, a soft banner at the top: "last check-in was Tuesday — two
  minutes?"
- **Home button/logo tap-to-return.** The header logo should scroll to top
  on tap (the About modal is nice, but "tap title to go home" is stronger
  muscle memory for lay users).

## Computer / desktop view

### What works
- **Desktop respects the 520px content column** — no overstretched
  layouts, stays readable.
- **About modal** is well-sized and feels intentional on a big screen.
- **Visualizers** (atom / fibonacci / etc) look great with a big canvas.

### What hurts on desktop
1. **Most of the screen is wasted** — at 1440px there's a 520px column in
   the middle and nothing on the sides. Feels like a phone app zoomed out.
   **Fix:** on ≥1024px, open a side rail on the *right* showing "today at a
   glance": yesterday's check-in, current streak, the binaural pill. The
   center column keeps the primary interaction; the rail is ambient.
2. **Keyboard shortcuts don't exist.** Desktop users expect `space` to
   play/pause sounds, `j/k` to nudge beat freq, `esc` to close modals.
   **Fix:** a tiny `useKeyboardShortcuts` hook wired to:
   - `space` toggle Calming Sounds play
   - `m` mute
   - `1-4` genre pick
   - `?` open a small shortcuts overlay
3. **Hover states are weak or missing.** Most buttons only invert on
   active — a proper hover highlight would make desktop feel less
   mobile-ported.
4. **The Day tabs** (Check in / Sounds) are narrow pills that look cramped
   in a wide window.
   **Fix:** on ≥1024px, split Check in | Sounds into a side-by-side
   two-pane layout instead of a tab switch. Both visible at once.

### What's missing on desktop
- **Keyboard shortcuts overlay** (see above).
- **Copy-to-clipboard** on the binaural readout so people can share
  "try 60Hz + 5Hz beat" with a friend.
- **Resizable wave** — on a big screen the 320×100 wave feels small. Let
  it scale to the column width.

## Cross-cutting / both views

### Mental-model clarity for lay people
- "**Binaural beat**" is jargon. 90% of users won't know what it means.
  Rename the shortcut text to "**brain-wave tuning**" or "**frequency
  bath**" and keep the Hz number as the subtitle. Geeks still see the
  numbers; everyone else reads something they can parse.
- "**Hawkins scale / Flow / Challenge**" — the names come from specific
  frameworks nobody outside the project knows. Either explain once in a
  first-run overlay, or rename to layman terms ("energy level" / "what's
  flowing" / "what's hard").
- "**Magic Maker**" is evocative but vague. Tooltip explaining "a visual
  synth — touch the grid to create melodies" when the user first taps it.
- "**Circles**" is overloaded (social + rituals + sharing). Either lean
  into "coworking circles" as the framing everywhere, or rename to "rooms"
  or "rituals."

### Onboarding
- Right now: the first load dumps you into CheckIn with no context.
- **Proposal:** a 3-step soft intro (≤30 seconds): welcome → what you'll
  do each day (check in + listen) → what builds up (the map). Skip button
  visible. Stored as `colourmap:onboarded=true`.

### Accessibility quick wins
- Aria-labels on genre/layer/wave-style pills (currently title= only).
- Focus rings. Several buttons remove them without providing a replacement
  — bad for keyboard nav.
- Color contrast on the `opacity: 0.3` faded text (e.g. "hide" on
  FrequencyBox). On bright screens it fails AA.
- `<button>` without `type="button"` defaults to submit — there are still
  a few of these in the codebase (check Magic Maker).

### Performance / perceived speed
- Calming Sounds takes ~200ms to boot the AudioContext — add an optimistic
  "tuning in..." label on the play button for that window so users don't
  double-tap.
- AtomVisualizer renders at 60fps even when tab is hidden on some
  browsers. Ensure `visibilitychange` pauses are respected everywhere.

## Priority ranking (what to ship next)

1. **First-run onboarding overlay** — biggest impact for lay users. ~1 day.
2. **Calming Sounds density collapse** (studio drawer) — second biggest. ~half day.
3. **Jargon pass** — rename binaural/Hawkins/Flow/Challenge to approachable
   terms in the *default* surface; keep the specialist terms in settings
   or tooltips. ~half day.
4. **Tab glyphs + logo-home tap** — polish. ~2 hours.
5. **Desktop side rail for the ≥1024px case** — makes the app feel at
   home on a laptop. ~1 day.
6. **Keyboard shortcuts + overlay** — desktop power user polish. ~half day.
7. **First-time check-in ping** ("two minutes?") — retention nudge. ~2 hours.
8. **Lo-fi beat bed *inside* Calming Sounds** — bridges the tuner/looper
   divide that today feels fragmented. ~1 day.
9. **Wire real piano/violin/flute/harp samples** to the Magic Maker
   melody engine with a sparse scheduler. Today those files exist on
   disk but are unused. ~1 day.
10. **Haptic wrapper** — subtle feel improvement, very cheap. ~2 hours.

## Answers to "where are things"

- **Shaman drums**: `BinauralTuner.tsx:843` — layers `real-shamanic-drum`
  and `real-tambourine` in the real-recording group, visible in Calming
  Sounds *studio mode*.
- **Real piano/violin/flute/harp samples**: files exist in
  `public/sounds/{piano,violin,flute,harp}/` but are not loaded by the
  app — the "Soft Piano / Harp / Strings" rows in the melody section are
  oscillator-synthesized (triangle / sine / sawtooth), not sampled.
- **Lo-fi beat maker**: lives in `SoundLab.tsx` as the **Lo-fi Looper**
  mode (separate from Calming Sounds). There's no embed into Calming
  Sounds today — proposed above.
- **Design polish / slider refinements**: landed across ~15 "refine:"
  commits on main over the last few days (rotary knobs, ochre palette,
  pastel pills, losange/dot/square Hawkins styles, bigger tap targets,
  bigger typography, rainbow sliders).
