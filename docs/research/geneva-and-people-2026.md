## 1 · Why Geneva

Switzerland is a quantitatively dominant global commodity-trading hub. Geneva is its centre of gravity.

- **CHF 19.2 billion** in value added from commodity trading in 2024 (~2.3% of Swiss total). [BFS / Swiss Federal Statistical Office, primary source.]
- Growth from **CHF 2bn (2002) → CHF 80bn (2022)** in net merchanting receipts (caveat: 2022 was a Russia-sanctions energy peak; comparing 2024 value added to 2022 net merchanting receipts is comparing different metrics — do not interpret the gap as a "decline").
- **900+ firms, ~10,000 employees** concentrated in Geneva (44%), Zug (21.4%), Ticino (9.5%).
- **>1/3 of global crude oil trade** handled from Switzerland (Swiss EDA, primary). 35% global oil-market share, ~700M tonnes/year via Geneva + Zug (WTI, Lindemann Law).
- **World leader** in sugar, cotton, oilseed, coffee, cereals. Public Eye estimates ≥50% global grain/coffee, 40% sugar, 30% cocoa, 25% cotton, 15% orange juice dispatched from Swiss desks.
- **Most goods never physically transit Switzerland.** This is merchanting/transit trade — desks routing physical flows between non-Swiss origins and destinations.

### Why the cluster formed in Geneva

Structural reasons accumulated over decades — most documented across Public Eye, Swiss EDA, Zug Commodities, and the SECO/SIF policy archive:

- **Tax structure** — favorable cantonal tax regimes (Zug particularly), legacy holding privileges (now reformed post-OECD), participation exemptions.
- **Neutrality** — desks can trade with sanctioned counterparts via long-tolerated grey lanes (now under more pressure post-2022).
- **Banking and trade finance** — UBS, Credit Suisse (legacy), BCGE, BNP Paribas Suisse — the financial plumbing to issue letters of credit at speed.
- **Talent density** — University of Geneva, IHEID, EPFL, plus the SUISSENÉGOCE Academy and HEC Lausanne.
- **Time zone** — overlaps Asia close and US open; the freight desks can trade Singapore-to-Houston in a single working day.
- **Regulatory secrecy (historically)** — beneficial-ownership disclosure was lighter than London or New York.

### Geneva vs the other shipping/trading capitals

| Capital | What it is | When you'd target it |
|---|---|---|
| **Geneva** | Commodity-trader + tanker-charterer + freight derivatives + ship finance + MSC HQ | Going where physical oil/dry-bulk volumes get priced & routed |
| **London** | Insurance (Lloyd's), shipbroking (Clarksons, Howe Robinson, Braemar), shipping P&I clubs, English law contracts | Insurance, dispute, finance |
| **Singapore** | Bunkering, Asia container hub, IMC, Hapag-Lloyd Asia office | Asia operations, bunkering, port-state work |
| **Hamburg** | Hapag-Lloyd HQ, NSB, Marquard & Bahls, container academia | German-speaking line management |
| **Marseille** | **CMA CGM HQ.** French maritime cluster, Tangram, Pulse | French/Mediterranean container ops, what we care about most |
| **Athens** | Greek dry-bulk and tanker owners, Posidonia biennial | Ship-owner side of the industry |

## 2 · SUISSENÉGOCE and the Geneva big players

### SUISSENÉGOCE (formerly STSA)

The Swiss Trading and Shipping Association renamed itself **SUISSENÉGOCE** in June 2023. It explicitly represents commodity trading **and shipping** as one integrated cluster: ~215 member companies, 8-9% of Swiss GDP. Maintains a dedicated Shipping Committee. ### MSC — Geneva-headquartered container giant

Verified only at the structural level in this pass:

- Geneva HQ. **#1 container carrier globally** at 7.136 M TEU as of Jan 2026 — pulled decisively ahead of Maersk.
- Aponte family ownership; generational handover from Gianluigi to Diego documented.
- Søren Toft has been CEO since 2020 (ex-Maersk COO). Lloyd's List 2024 piece "MSC chief Soren Toft: We have the scale and strength to stand alone" frames the post-2M-alliance positioning.

## 5 · The ask strategy for a young builder

The research crystallises the ask strategy into three operating rules:

### Rule 1 — Pitch the product, not the CV

The refuted "internships open to all disciplines" claim points the way. CMA CGM does not appear to use generic-CV channels as a meaningful pipeline for digital/AI/product roles. Where they *do* engage early-career talent — Startup Awards, ZEBOX, HEC challenges — they ask for **a thing built**, not a CV.

For an Albert School master's student, this means: the canonical artifact is the proposal page (`/proposal/cma-cgm` — see [cma-cgm-product.md](../specs/cma-cgm-product.md)) plus a running prototype, not a 1-page CV plus a cover letter.

### Rule 2 — Use HEC's channel as the demonstrated case

The September 2025 HEC × CMA CGM AI Challenge is the cleanest demonstration of the pipeline. 32 students, 6 teams, 1 week, AI-powered business intelligence for logistics — judged personally by the Head of AI and the Head of Marketing. The pipeline is real.

Two paths to enter it:

- **Direct route:** approach **Jean Fauquembergue** (CMA CGM Head of AI) and **Agnès Mossina** (Head of Marketing & Studies) cold — they are publicly identifiable, named, and *demonstrably engage with student-stage builders*. Cold-outreach response rates are unknown, but the engagement surface is real.
- **Institutional route:** if Albert School has any AI/digital partnership with HEC, leverage that to get on a similar challenge. If not, propose one to Albert School's industry team using the HEC case as the template.

### Rule 3 — Go through ZEBOX with a working product

[ZEBOX](https://www.ze-box.io/startup-programs) is the documented official channel. Ignite track explicitly accepts MVP/prototype stage. Scale-up track is for revenue-stage startups. The page verbatim: "gateway to collaboration with the CMA CGM Group."

For a young builder, the realistic Ignite pitch is:

- A working prototype of the Geopolitics Intel module (V1 page reader + a CMA-CGM-skinned daily brief).
- A working prototype of SeafarerNote (one onboard module on a phone).
- A 6-week pilot proposal scoped against an actual CMA CGM ship.

### Concrete past-winner study

The 2024 Startup Awards winners are public — track them as the tonal signal for "what CMA CGM picks":

- **Aerleum** — direct air capture, carbon removal.
- **Elonroad** — inductive road charging for trucks/EVs.
- **GBMS** — quantum-resistant cybersecurity.
- **ZERO44** — vessel emissions software for carriers (this is the closest to a Geopolitics Intel cousin).
- **Okular Logistics** — computer vision for warehouse / port operations.
- **Optioryx** — AI cargo loading optimisation.
- **Argil** — generative AI for content (likely media-side).

The set is broad. CMA CGM is picking across decarbonisation, ops AI, cybersecurity, and media — not narrowly maritime. **Implication:** an intel/wellbeing twin-product is well within the pattern.
