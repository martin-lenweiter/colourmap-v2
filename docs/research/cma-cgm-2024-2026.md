| Group EBITDA | **$13.4 bn** (24.2% margin) | **$10.6 bn** (19.4% margin) | |

| Other Activities revenue | – | **$4.3 bn** (+48%) | Terminals + air cargo + media |

[Confirmed 3-0 across both years. Source: CMA CGM annual results pages, corroborated by AJOT, Container News, Port Technology, DredgeWire, Hellenic Shipping News.]

### What this means for the pitch

The group is not in distress. It is in disciplined diversification. The pitch tone should be: "I am bringing you something that compounds where you are already deploying capital" — not "I am bringing you something that saves you from a problem."

## 2 · CEVA Logistics — the second pillar

**$18.3 bn revenue in 2025**, **top-4 logistics operator globally** (DSV → DHL → Kuehne+Nagel → CEVA).

- **Bolloré Logistics acquisition closed 29 February 2024.** Largest acquisition in CMA CGM group history. Cemented CEVA's position as the major non-DSV/DHL/K+N alternative.
- **Mathieu Friedberg** is CEVA Logistics CEO.
- **Warehouse footprint:** ~10.3 million sqm (referenced in the Google Cloud partnership scope).

CEVA is the *natural integration partner* for any product that touches container/warehouse/multimodal — and the natural commercial pilot host if the Intel module needs an enterprise testbed.

## 3 · Media — the third pillar

CMA CGM has spent ~€1.55 bn building France's self-described **third-largest private media group** in less than two years.

| Acquisition | Period | What |
|---|---|---|
| La Provence, Corse Matin | pre-2024 | Regional print |
| La Tribune, La Tribune Dimanche | pre-2024 | Business print |
| **Altice Media** | Cleared by Autorité de la concurrence 28 June 2024; closed July 2024; ~**€1.55 bn** | **BFM TV, BFM Business TV, RMC Découverte, RMC Story**, 10 local channels |
| Brut | Sept 2025 | Mobile/social video |
| Cherie 25 | Oct 2025 | TV channel |

[Verified 3-0. Sources: Autorité de la concurrence press release, tvbeurope.com, Wikipedia CMA Media entry, L'Orient Today, La Releve.]

**Behavioural remedies** were imposed by the regulator because La Provence's geographic overlap with local BFM channels raised concentration concerns — a useful detail for any product that touches media-distribution narratives.

### Implication for the pitch

CMA CGM's media holdings are the **third-largest private French media group** — meaning the company *owns its own distribution surface*. A CMA-CGM-skinned daily intel brief can be syndicated through BFM Business and La Tribune with no third-party gatekeeper. This is structurally enormous, and the Geopolitics Platform's *Visual Language and Storytelling Hooks* section should anticipate this distribution surface from day one.

## 4 · Terminals — the asset spine

- **$2.5 bn deployed in 2025** on terminal investment.
- **66 terminals across 40 countries** (CMA Terminals + JVs).
- Christine Cabau Woehrel's portfolio includes (verified): **Sokhna Red Sea Container Terminal (1.7 M TEU, JV with Hutchison Ports + COSCO Shipping Ports)**; **Jeddah Terminal 4 ($450 M JV with RSGT, signed at FII9)**; **Mombasa modernization ($820 M under France-Kenya cooperation framework, signed at Africa Forward Summit)**.

(Note: the Mombasa figure varies slightly across sources — $756 M reported by Food Business MEA, $820 M by Maritime Executive and Business Daily Africa. Treat as **directional ~$800 M** until disambiguated.)

The terminals are where geopolitical risk meets the income statement most directly. A war-risk event in Hormuz, Red Sea, or Africa changes throughput at named terminals on this list — Sokhna, Jeddah, Mombasa are all geographically in-frame.

## 5 · Decarbonisation — the **$20 bn** thesis

- **~$20 bn fleet decarbonisation envelope** committed.
- **12 LNG vessel deliveries in 2024.**
- **27 LNG + methanol vessel deliveries in 2025.**
- Target: **153 low-carbon-capable vessels by 2029.**
- Target: **200+ vessels by 2030.**

[Verified 3-0. Sources: 2024 and 2025 annual results, corroborated AJOT, DredgeWire, Container News, MAN-ES decarbonisation case study, Maritime Executive, Sustainability Magazine.]

### Methodological caveat

Adversarial sources (Say No to LNG, The Loadstar) accept the dollar figures and ship counts but contest whether LNG counts as "low-carbon" on methane-slip grounds. The numerical commitments are not disputed; the **labelling** is. In platform content, present the numbers crisply and note the methane-slip debate as a *Reflection — what to improve next* item rather than burying it.

### Order book specifics — partially confirmed

- **TradeWinds** reported a **methanol → LNG switch** on some new dual-fuel boxship orders (LNGPrime corroborates, Maritime Executive partially corroborates with a $2 bn Hyundai-linked methanol order).
- Named ships, yards (CSSC vs Hyundai HI vs Samsung HI), and ammonia order book specifics: **open** — not surfaced as a verified anchor set.

This is a known watch item for any Intel product touching the carrier order book.

## What this changes for the pitch

Quoting the four verified pieces of high-signal Saadé/CMA CGM positioning, in order of usefulness:

1. **"I would like the younger generation to benefit from all the opportunities that this technology has to offer."** Lead the pitch email with this — verbatim — and frame the project as a direct answer to that ask.
2. **€500 m AI commitment + Kyutai/Mistral/AMI Labs/Google Cloud stack.** The pitch's technical credibility relies on understanding this stack and mapping the product into it (Mistral as the LLM substrate for the daily-brief drafter, French-AI sovereignty as the trust frame).

4. **42 Marseille named case.** The closest verified template for "how does a 22-year-old get inside CMA CGM with a built thing." Study the Angelo Gabriel Mikael case before drafting.
